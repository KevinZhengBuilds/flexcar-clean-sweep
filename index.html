The 8 stations

    From CA1-WASH-01, Two Person Car Wash SOP v1.3 (San Jose Hub). Pilot target: 8 to 9 minutes for a normally dirty sedan.

    Your side

    ADriver side, left half of roof, glass, hood and trunk, plus the full front bumper and grille. BPassenger side, right half of roof, glass, hood and trunk, plus the full rear bumper.

    Split every shared panel at the centerline. No overlap, no missed areas.

    How you finish

    Each station's gate stays locked until you've collected every SOP step there, 28 steps in all. The dirty flag comes down only after the last quality-check step. Then raise the clean Flexcar flag and meet your partner at the car for handoff.
